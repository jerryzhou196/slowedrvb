slowedrvb chrome extension privacy policy

last updated: october 18, 2026

the slowedrvb extension processes audio entirely inside your browser. it does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

  • it only runs on a tab after you open the extension popup on that tab.
  • the audio effects (speed, reverb, bass, 8d, pan, volume) are applied locally with the Web Audio API. no audio leaves your computer.
  • effect settings only live in the tab you set them on and are gone when you reload or close it. nothing is stored.
  • the extension has no analytics, ads, accounts or remote code.

questions: open an issue on github · back to slowedrvb.com

website download analytics

This section applies to the slowedrvb website, not the Chrome extension. When you request a YouTube download, we use a random, first-party browser cookie refreshed for 30 days after each download to estimate distinct downloading browsers. It is not a login, a fingerprint, or a count of individual people.

For 30 days, the download service stores the requested video ID and title, request time, result, transfer size, and keyed hashes of the browser identifier and network address. Raw IP addresses are not stored in this analytics database. The private owner dashboard shows download history, hourly and daily usage, distinct browsers, and distinct addresses. Shared networks, VPNs, changing addresses, private browsing and cookie clearing affect these estimates.

The audio itself is temporary and is deleted after transfer, failure or cancellation. Playing a previously saved browser copy does not send a new download request and is not counted here.
